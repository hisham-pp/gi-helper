# Repository Guidelines

## Project Structure & Module Organization

This is a Next.js 15 App Router project with React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.

- **Application Routes (`src/app/`):**
  - `src/app/(site)/layout.tsx`: Shared layout for the public pages; renders the common top bar (`src/components/layout/SiteHeader.tsx`), which stays mounted across page switches. On phones the page links (`HeaderNav`) take their own full-width row under the logo. Add new public pages inside `(site)`.
  - `src/app/(site)/template.tsx`: Remounts on every navigation and wraps the page in `PageTransition` (`src/components/layout/`), which plays the squash-and-stretch page switch. The `db/` and `inventory/` templates nest a second one around just the page: switches inside the section animate only that (the sub-nav stays still), entering the section animates it all.
  - `src/app/(site)/page.tsx`: Landing page containing the Inventory Scanner (Scanner, Video and AI Vision tabs). The Scanner and Video tabs read materials, weapons, artifacts and the party setup's characters; results go to the `/inventory` page of what was found most, so it has no extra bottom space; the footer shows the scan counter (`ScanCounter`) here.
  - `src/app/(site)/inventory/`: Inventory sub-pages `/inventory/materials`, `/characters`, `/weapons`, `/artifacts` (`/inventory` redirects to the first). `layout.tsx` renders `InventoryShell` (`src/components/inventory/`): the header with GOOD import (`ImportGOODModal`) and GOOD export of everything (which offers the feedback form, throttled by `src/lib/feedback/prompt.ts`), and links to the four pages with counts; it stays mounted between them, `template.tsx` animates the page, and the pages reach it through `useInventoryActions()`. The Materials page is `src/components/scanner/ScannedInventory.tsx`, given the catalogs without descriptions by its server page (quantity edits, Clear All, plus imported materials we don't catalog). The scanners add materials to the shared store (`src/lib/scanner-inventory.ts`) and switch here after a scan; characters, weapons and artifacts live in `src/lib/good-inventory.ts` (light stores, also used by the database pages; applying an import is `src/lib/good-import.ts`, saving scanned ones is `saveScannedEquipment` in `src/components/scanner/equipment-review.ts`: weapons and artifacts replace or add per the review's checkbox, characters merge by key and keep constellations and talents). Scanned artifacts other than flowers and plumes have no main stat until it's picked (review or the Artifacts page); GOOD export leaves them out (`hasMainStat`).
  - `src/app/(site)/db/`: Database sub-pages `/db/materials`, `/db/characters`, `/db/weapons`, `/db/artifacts` (`/db` redirects to the first; the old `/materials` etc. redirect permanently, see `next.config.ts` `redirects()`). `layout.tsx` holds the page padding and `DatabaseNav`, which stays mounted between them; `template.tsx` animates the page. Materials is `MaterialList` (both catalogs: Character Development Items and the Materials tab, with category filtering and ordering), given the catalogs without descriptions by its server page; descriptions load on demand from `/api/material-descriptions` (`src/lib/material-descriptions.ts`). The others are `src/components/database/` (`CharacterList`, `WeaponList`, `ArtifactSetList`), marking what's in the inventory. The top bar's "Database" and "Inventory" links (`HeaderNav`, `PillNavItem.match`) are current on all of their section's pages.
  - `src/app/(site)/versions/page.tsx`: Release notes page, built from `src/data/versions/` at build time. Shows the newest 10 (`RELEASES_PER_PAGE` in `src/lib/release-notes.ts`); `OlderReleases` loads 10 more at a time from `/api/versions/[page]` (static JSON generated at build), and follows `#v0-0-2` links to older releases.
  - `src/app/(site)/about/page.tsx`: About page (features, how it works, privacy, fan-project disclaimer), linked from the shared footer (`SiteFooter`).
  - `src/app/(site)/feedback/page.tsx`: Public feedback list with the Send feedback modal (feedback, bug, idea or other; a name that starts as a random character's, with their avatar; up to 3 screenshots). Only the "feedback" kind is listed; `feedback/inbox/page.tsx` is the private inbox for seeded accounts. Linked from the footer and About page. See `docs/feedback.md`.
  - `src/app/api/feedback/`: Feedback route handlers (list and send, images, inbox session, inbox list/update/delete).
  - `src/app/api/stats/scans/route.ts`: Scan counter: GET totals (CDN-cached for a minute), POST `{ scanner }` after a scan.
  - `src/app/api/scanner/ai-vision/route.ts`: Server Route Handler for Vision AI calls (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Grok, Custom) and connection testing.
  - `src/app/debug/`: Testing utilities and debug exploration views.
- **Components (`src/components/`):**
  - `src/components/scanner/`: Inventory scanner UI: local on-device scanner (`ClassicScannerView.tsx`), screen-recording scanner (`VideoScannerView.tsx`), AI Vision scanner (`AiScannerView.tsx`), drag-and-drop zone (`UploadZone.tsx`), multi-provider settings (`ScannerSettingsModal.tsx`), review modal (`ScanReviewModal.tsx`, with a tab per kind; weapon, artifact and character rows are `EquipmentReviewRow.tsx`, reassigned with `EquipmentPickerModal.tsx`), and the scanned-inventory cards (`ResultGrid.tsx`, used by `ScannedInventory.tsx` on `/inventory/materials`).
  - `src/components/grid-detector/`: Grid & Material Studio (`/debug`): sessions, card editor, detection settings.
  - `src/components/material-detector/`: Recognition panels used by the studio (per-card candidates, rarity and quantity readings).
  - `src/components/materials/`: Material directory cards, category filters, and detail modals.
  - `src/components/inventory/`: `InventoryShell` (the /inventory section's header and links), its Characters, Weapons and Artifacts pages, and the GOOD import modal.
  - `src/components/layout/`: Navigation bar, theme toggles, and header controls. `PillNav` is the glass pill of page links with a sliding thumb (optional count badges, `match` for a section's sub-pages), used by the top bar (`HeaderNav`), the footer (`FooterNav`) and the section sub-navs (`DatabaseNav`, `InventoryShell`). In a flex column give it `self-start` (or `shrink-0` in a scrolling row) so it keeps its natural width.
  - `src/components/ui/`: Reusable primitives and badges. Text fields use `GlassInput` (leading icon, `clearable`, built-in show/hide for `type="password"`, `shape`/`size`); `SearchField` wraps it. Don't hand-build inputs with absolutely positioned buttons. Squash-and-stretch on change comes from `useWiggleOnChange` / `playWiggle` (`src/lib/use-wiggle-on-change.ts`, sizes `card` and `control`); `PillDropdown` and `GlassInput` use it.
- **Shared Logic & AI Engine (`src/lib/`):**
  - `src/lib/ai/adapters/`: Pluggable AI vision adapters implementing the `AiVisionAdapter` interface for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Grok, and Custom/OpenRouter.
  - `src/lib/ai/material-matcher.ts`: Exact and fuzzy Levenshtein resolver mapping raw OCR strings to canonical materials.
  - `src/lib/ai/prompts.ts`: In-prompt reference dictionary generator embedding canonical material IDs into AI system prompts.
  - `src/lib/scanner-settings.ts`: Local storage persistence and react hooks for scanner settings.
  - `src/lib/good/`: GOOD (Genshin Open Object Description) format: keys (`toGOODKey`, the same PascalCase rule for materials, characters, weapons and sets), types and stat names, lenient parsing (`document.ts`, matching Genshin Optimizer's schema, GOOD 3 fields kept), export (`export.ts`), GOOD material keys to our catalogs (`materials.ts`), and catalog lookups (`catalog.ts`: weapons, artifact sets, characters by GOOD key; unknown keys keep working). `src/lib/good-format.ts` has the download helpers.
  - `src/lib/local-store.ts`: `createLocalStore`, the shared localStorage-backed store behind the inventory stores.
  - `src/lib/grid-detector/`: Card/grid detection engine (pure TS) with a Web Worker client. See `docs/grid-detector.md`.
  - `src/lib/material-detector/`: On-device card recognition of detected cards, with a Web Worker scan client: materials (rarity, quantity), weapons (level, refinement, lock, awakened icon), artifacts (set and slot, rarity, level, lock) and the party setup's characters (level). Each grid's kind comes from the text under its cards (`engine/kind.ts`), so only that kind's atlas loads. See `docs/material-detector.md`.
  - `src/lib/scan-equipment.ts`: Weapons, artifacts and characters between the recognizer and the inventory: rows stitched across overlapping screenshots and video frames (they repeat, so they aren't merged by id), ascensions, GOOD entries.
  - `src/lib/video-scanner/`: Screen-recording scanner: picks the frames where scrolling settles, scans them with the material scan workers, fills coverage gaps, merges quantities by vote and stitches weapon, artifact and character rows. See `docs/video-scanner.md`.
  - `src/lib/supabase-rpc.ts`: `callRpc` for the `public.*` Postgres functions (publishable key, server-only), shared by feedback and the scan counter; `src/lib/request-client.ts` has `clientIp`, `isSameOrigin` and the salted `hashClient`.
  - `src/lib/scan-stats.ts`: Scan counter. The scanners call `recordScan(scanner, files)` once a scan recognises materials; files from the "Try a sample" buttons (`markSampleFile` in `UploadZone`) don't count. Server side in `scan-stats-server.ts`. The database broadcasts every new total on the public Supabase Realtime channel `scan-stats`; `scan-stats-live.ts` listens (WebSocket, home page only, closed while the tab is hidden), using the URL and publishable key that `GET /api/stats/scans` returns.
  - `src/lib/characters.ts`: Playable characters (`getCharacter`, `randomCharacter`, `characterAvatarUrl`), used for feedback names and avatars and, by GOOD key, in the inventory.
  - `src/lib/feedback/`: Feedback server calls (`server.ts`, Supabase RPCs with the publishable key), shared types and limits, the `bad-words` filter (`profanity.ts`) and image checks. See `docs/feedback.md`.
- **Database (`supabase/migrations/`):** Supabase project `gi-helper`. Feedback tables live in the unexposed `feedback_private` schema and are reached only through the `public.feedback_*` security-definer functions; the scan counter likewise uses `stats_private` via `public.scan_stats` / `public.scan_record` (20 counted scans per sender and 600 overall per 10 minutes). Inbox accounts are seeded with `pnpm feedback:admin <username>` (bcrypt hash, no Supabase Auth). Server env: `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, optional `FEEDBACK_HASH_SALT`, and `SUPABASE_RPC_SECRET`: the publishable key is public (Realtime), so the write functions (`feedback_submit`, `feedback_admin_login`, `scan_record`) check this server-only secret (`app_private.settings`) once `enforce_rpc_secret` is on.
- **Data & Static Assets:**
  - `src/data/materials.json`: Canonical reference data for the 481 Character Development Items (served at `/api/materials`). The scanners, recognizer atlas and AI prompt use only this (`getAllMaterials()`).
  - `src/data/other-materials.json`: The bag's Materials tab (local specialties, cooking ingredients, fish and bait, ores, wood, crafting materials; `category` and `region` fields, orders from 10001, `bagOrder()` shows their place in that tab). Built by `pnpm scrape:other-materials`, thumbnails in `material-thumbs`. The directory, inventory and GOOD import use both catalogs (`getDirectoryMaterials()`).
  - `src/data/versions/<major>-<minor>-<patch>/release.json`: Release notes per version (`{ title, date: "YYYY-MM-DD", summary?, changes: [{ type: "new" | "improved" | "fixed" | "removed", text }] }`). The folder name is the version; add a folder to publish a release. Loaded and validated by `src/lib/versions.ts`.
  - `public/images/materials/`: High-resolution icons for all 475 materials.
  - `public/samples/test-set-02/`: Sample inventory screen recording, used by the Video tab's "Try a sample recording" button.
  - Brand icons: `src/app/favicon.ico`, `src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png` and `public/icons/*.png` are all generated from one master image, `scripts/brand/icon-source.png` (a rounded tile on white), by `pnpm build:brand-icons`. To change the icon, replace the source and re-run; don't edit the outputs. `BrandIcon` shows it in the UI. The manifest is `src/app/manifest.ts`, built from `src/lib/site.ts`.
  - `public/images/material-thumbs/`: 128² WebP icons (~4 KB each) for lists, grids and pickers, via `materialThumbUrl()` / `MaterialThumb` (generated by `pnpm build:material-thumbs`, also run by `sync-data`).
  - `public/data/<kind>-atlas.{webp,json}`: The recognizer's reference banks, one per kind (material: both bag tabs; weapon: base, awakened and alternative icons; artifact: every set × slot; character: avatars), lossless WebP, generated by `pnpm build:atlases` (the last step of `sync-data`).
  - `tests/online-samples/`: Players' weapon, artifact, Materials-tab and character-list screenshots used to test and measure the scanner. Only the manifest (URLs) and the labels (`ground-truth.json`) are in the repository: `pnpm fetch:online-samples` downloads the images into the gitignored `files/`, and their tests are skipped without them. `pnpm eval:card-scanner` reports accuracy.
  - `src/data/digit-templates.json`: Quantity digit glyphs (generated by `pnpm build:digit-templates`).
  - `src/data/characters.json` and `public/images/character-avatars/<id>.webp`: Playable characters (id, GOOD key, name, rarity, element, weapon type) and their 128² WebP avatars (~8 KB each; only the WebP is stored). Generated from genshin-db and enka.network icons by `pnpm scrape:characters` (also run by `sync-data`).
  - `src/data/weapons.json` + `public/images/weapons/<id>.webp` and `src/data/artifact-sets.json` + `public/images/artifacts/<set-id>-<slot>.webp`: Weapons (GOOD key, rarity, type) and artifact sets (GOOD key, rarities, piece names) with 128² WebP icons, from `pnpm scrape:weapons` / `pnpm scrape:artifacts` (also run by `sync-data`). `scrape:weapons` also saves each weapon's awakened icon (`public/images/weapons-awakened/`) and HoYoLAB's copy where it frames the icon differently from enka.network (`public/images/weapons-alt/`; the game shows those), both only for the scanner's atlas. The scrapers share `scripts/lib/scrape-helpers.ts`.

## Build, Test, and Development Commands

Prefer `pnpm`; the repo pins `pnpm@10.17.1` and Node `22.x`.

- `pnpm dev`: Start the local development server at `http://localhost:3000` with Turbopack (pages compile in well under a second; `pnpm dev:webpack` for the old bundler). Dev mode ships unminified code: judge page speed on a production build (`pnpm build && pnpm start`).
- `pnpm build`: Create an optimized production build.
- `pnpm start`: Start the production server.
- `pnpm test`: Run unit tests using Node's built-in test runner (`tsx --test tests/**/*.test.ts`).
- `pnpm test:material-detector` / `pnpm eval:material-detector`: Material recognizer tests and accuracy report.
- `pnpm fetch:online-samples` / `pnpm eval:card-scanner`: Download players' weapon, artifact, Materials-tab and character screenshots (`tests/online-samples/`) / the scanner's accuracy on them.
- `pnpm build:atlases [material|weapon|artifact|character]`: Rebuild the scanner's reference atlases from the icons (after a scrape; `sync-data` does it last).
- `pnpm test:grid-detector` / `pnpm eval:grid-detector`: Grid detector tests and accuracy report.
- `pnpm scrape:characters` / `scrape:weapons` / `scrape:artifacts` / `scrape:other-materials`: Rebuild the character, weapon, artifact-set and Materials-tab data and icons (update genshin-db first when new ones release). Icons come from enka.network, falling back to gi.yatta.moe.
- `pnpm test:e2e`: Playwright smoke tests (`tests/e2e/`) against the production build on port 3100; run `pnpm build` first. `pnpm dev` writes to `.next` too, so while it runs, build and test into another folder: `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-prod` for both commands (`$env:NEXT_DIST_DIR=".next-prod"` in PowerShell). `next-env.d.ts` is regenerated by every build and isn't committed.
- `pnpm feedback:admin <username> [--generate]`: Prints the SQL that seeds or resets a feedback inbox account (run it in the Supabase SQL editor).
- `pnpm lint`: Run ESLint with Next.js core web vitals configuration.
- `pnpm format`: Run Prettier across the repository.

## Coding Style & Architectural Conventions

- **TypeScript Throughout:** Maintain strict TypeScript types. Domain types are defined in `src/lib/types.ts` and `src/lib/ai/adapters/types.ts`.
- **AI Vision Adapters:** When adding or updating AI models, implement the `AiVisionAdapter` contract in `src/lib/ai/adapters/` and register them in `src/lib/ai/adapters/index.ts`.
- **Precedence Rule:** User API keys in `localStorage` must always take precedence over server `.env` variables.
- **Components:** Functional React 19 components in `PascalCase`. Route segments and helper modules use `kebab-case`.
- **Icons & Styling:** Use `lucide-react` for icons and Tailwind CSS with glassmorphic, theme-aware classes. Press effects on glass controls use the `scale` property, not `transform`, so they stack with positioning transforms (a `transform` there once made pressed buttons jump away from the pointer).
- **Light/Dark Theme:** Tailwind's `white` is the theme ink (`--ink`: white in dark, slate in light), so `text-white`, `bg-white/10` and `border-white/10` adapt automatically; use `snow` for anything that must stay white. Wrap intentionally dark surfaces (scan chamber, dark banners) in `on-dark` to keep white ink and pale accents there. Light versions of the glass classes live in `src/styles/glass-light.css`; pale accent text (`text-cyan-300`, ...) is remapped to deep shades in light mode in `globals.css`.
- **Modals:** Load modals with `next/dynamic` (`ssr: false`) and mount them only while open (see `src/components/scanner/lazy-modals.ts`); call `usePauseBackground()` inside so the animated background stops under the blur. Use `useModalMotion(onClose)` (`src/lib/use-modal-motion.ts`) for the open/close squash-and-stretch: attach its `dialogRef`/`backdropRef` and close through its `close`/`closeThen` instead of calling `onClose` directly. Toasts are styled by `GlassToaster` (`src/components/ui/`); test toasts and modals from the Motion Playground on `/debug`. Never use the browser's `confirm()`/`alert()`: use `useConfirm()` (`src/components/ui/use-confirm.tsx`), which shows the glass `ConfirmModal`. Page loaders: each public page has its own skeleton in `PAGE_SHELLS` (`src/components/layout/PageShells.tsx`, same hero and layout as the page), shown by `PageShellSlot` in the `(site)` layout only while a `ShellLink`'s page is really loading (the link is pending and the router is fetching that page from the server, not a prefetch). `ShellLink` prefetches the full page and `PageShellSlot` preloads every page in `PAGE_SHELLS` when the browser is idle, so the shell is rare. Link between public pages with `ShellLink` (`src/components/layout/`), not `next/link`, and add a shell for a new page. Don't add a `loading.tsx` above static pages: its placeholder goes into their prerendered HTML and shows before the page on every first visit. Only `feedback/inbox/loading.tsx` (rendered on request) uses one, with the generic `PageLoading`.
- **Caching:** `next.config.ts` `headers()` gives `public/` files real browser/CDN lifetimes (the hashed atlas is immutable; icons, avatars and samples a week + stale-while-revalidate). Public reads from Supabase go through Next's data cache (`unstable_cache` in `src/lib/feedback/server.ts` and `src/lib/scan-stats-server.ts`) and are dropped with `revalidateTag` when they change (new public post, hide/show, delete, recorded scan); inbox reads are never cached. Editing feedback directly in SQL bypasses that: the public page catches up within 10 minutes.
- **Preserve Documentation:** Keep existing comments intact.

## Testing Guidelines

- When modifying the AI vision pipeline or material matcher, add or update tests in `tests/ai-vision.test.ts`.
- When modifying the video scanner's frame selection or merging, update `tests/video-scanner.test.ts` and re-check the sample recording in the Video tab (expected numbers in `docs/video-scanner.md`).
- When modifying the material recognizer, keep `pnpm test:material-detector` green and check `pnpm eval:material-detector` (also with `--impostors` and `--scale 0.5`); per-card ground truth is in `tests/material-detector/ground-truth.json`. For weapons, artifacts, the Materials tab and characters, run `pnpm fetch:online-samples` once, then keep `tests/card-scanner.test.ts` green and check `pnpm eval:card-scanner`; stitching and GOOD conversion are in `tests/scan-equipment.test.ts`.
- When modifying GOOD parsing or export, the GOOD keys or the weapon/artifact/character data, update `tests/good-document.test.ts` (and `tests/e2e/fixtures/account.good.json` for the import flow).
- When modifying the feedback filter, image checks or error messages, update `tests/feedback.test.ts`; `tests/characters.test.ts` checks the character list and avatars, `tests/scan-stats.test.ts` the scan counter's sample skipping and scanner kinds. Schema changes go in a new file under `supabase/migrations/`.
- Always verify changes with `pnpm build` and `npx tsx --test tests/ai-vision.test.ts`.
